# Aura Cycles — brand direction (Aug 2026)

Rebrand from **Cycle Bae** → **Aura Cycles**.

## The Aura Engine
The deterministic backend (`engine.py`) is branded as **The Aura Engine**.
Positioning: it does not *guess* where you are in your cycle — it *computes* your capacity. The
engine is the trust layer; the brand is the face on it.

## Four pillars
| Pillar | Brand name | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Diet | **Aura Fuel** | Precise nutritional inputs to prevent an energy dip / "crash out" per stretch of the cycle |
| Fitness | **Aura Gains** | Heavy work when capacity peaks; strategic rest when the engine predicts low capacity |
| Social | **Aura Radiance** | High-energy gatherings in high-aura stretches; low-key/intimate settings in luteal & menstrual |
| Cycle | **The Cycle** | The foundational clock driving the whole deterministic equation |

## Core output
- **Aura Score** — daily capacity number instead of just "you are in X stretch."

## In-app vocabulary
- **Daily Aura Report** — morning push summarizing physical / emotional / social capacity for the day.
- **Aura Drain Warning** — alert that a planned workout/social event will likely drain energy given the current stretch. *(strong — concrete + useful)*
- **Aura Buffs** — quick daily tasks (e.g. "eat these healthy fats today"). *(see note below on the numbers)*

## Tagline candidates

3. "Data-driven cycle tracking for peak lifestyle alignment."

## Notes & open risks
- **Trademark:** "Aura" in health/wearables is crowded and dominated by **ŌURA**
  (the smart ring company). "Aura Cycles" in a cycle/health app sits close enough
  to their mark to be a real clearance question. Worth a quick knock-out search
  (USPTO + app store) before committing to the name in the store listing.
- **"Aura Buffs" with numeric scores** (e.g. "+50 Luteal-stretch Aura Buff") risks
  reading as fake-scientific gamification and can drift into wellness-claim
  territory — which cuts against the "deterministic / trust" positioning. Keep
  buffs as *named actions* ("eat these fats today"), drop the fake precision of
  +50 numbers.
- Existing code already has poetic stretch names (`Rest & Renew`, `Follicular Rise`,
  `Ovulation Glow`, `Luteal Flow`, `Reflect & Rest`) — these survive the rebrand
  unchanged and actually fit the Aura voice.
